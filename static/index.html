01 Oversight

Delegation needs oversight.

Hospitals and pension funds run on people handing work to others they can’t watch. Three old ideas make that safe.

  • A record.Every entry is written twice, so anyone can check the work afterwards.Double-entry bookkeeping, printed in Venice in 1494
  • A price.Watching the work, proving it was done right, and the losses that get through anyway. Companies pay it in approvals, reviews and audits.Agency costs, Jensen and Meckling, 1976
  • A signature.When a clerk approves a claim, a manager still signs for it. Whoever signs answers for the work, so they have to be able to see it.Executives sign their financial reports: Sarbanes-Oxley, 2002

Agents are a new kind of delegate.
