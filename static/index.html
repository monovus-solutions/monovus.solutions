How Monovus Launch works

Why agent projects get cancelled

The three reasons agent projects fail are all fixable.

Gartner expects over 40% of agentic AI projects to be cancelled by the end of 2027, because of escalating costs, unclear business value or inadequate risk controls. None of the three is a model problem, and each has a known fix.

Source: Gartner press release, 25 June 2025

Cost

Escalating costs

Usage grows faster than value, and nobody can say what one conversation costs.

How we remove it. We measure cost per task, set budgets with alarms and route simple turns to smaller models.

Value

Unclear business value

The pilot impressed people, but no one owns a number it has to move.

How we remove it. We start with a named owner and one measurable target, against today's baseline.

Risk

Inadequate risk controls

No one can prove what the agent can touch, or show what it did and why.

How we remove it. Six controls: evaluations, tracing, policy, identity, rollback and an evidence pack.

What we won't do.

Questions

What leaders ask us first.

Something else? Email hello@monovus.solutions.

Do we need to be on AWS?

Yes, or be willing to run the agent there. We work only with AWS services, in your own account. If your agent lives on another cloud, we are the wrong choice and we will say so.

Who owns the work?

You do. Everything is built in your AWS account and your repositories, under your IAM. When we finish, your team can run it without us.

What does the agent cost to run?

Amazon bills your account directly for usage, and we never mark it up. Part of the work is measuring cost per task and setting budgets with alarms, so you know the number before you scale.

How long does it take?

The assessment takes about five minutes and the Readiness Review 45. A Pilot-to-Production Sprint typically runs 2 to 4 weeks on one agent.

What if our agent isn't ready for production?

Then the scorecard says so, in writing, with what would have to change. Sometimes the right answer is a narrower agent, and we would rather tell you that than sell you a sprint.

Where does our data live?

In your AWS account, in the Region you choose. We work through an IAM role you create and can revoke at any time, and we don't copy your data into systems of our own.

Can you work with our existing team or AWS partner?

Yes. Your engineers work alongside us so the capability stays with you. AWS partners and MSPs can bring us in under their own contract.

Do you build new agents?

Yes, when the scope is one clear task. Most of our work takes an existing pilot to production; a scoped single-task agent is part of Production Build and Hardening.
