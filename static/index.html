Society

Delegation needs oversight.

Water systems, hospitals and pension funds run on people handing work to others they can’t watch. Signatures, job descriptions, ledgers and audits make that safe.

Double-entry bookkeeping was first printed in 1494.

Companies

Oversight has a price.

Economists call it agency costs: watching the work, proving it was done right, and the losses that get through anyway. Most companies pay it in approvals, reviews and audits.

Jensen and Meckling, 1976

People

Responsibility stays with people.

When a clerk approves a claim, a manager still answers for it. The people who answer for the work have to be able to see it.

Agents

Agents are a new kind of delegate.

They read what your company knows, decide and act: a refund, a price change, an invoice. They work at software speed, with none of the oversight built for people.

15%of day-to-day work decisions made autonomously by agentic AI by 2028, up from almost none in 2024.Gartner, October 2024

40%+of agentic AI projects expected to be cancelled by the end of 2027.Gartner, June 2025

Economics

Worth what it saves, minus what it costs to oversee.

Change the numbers. When a person checks every action, oversight eats the saving. When the system enforces the rules, people review only the exceptions.
