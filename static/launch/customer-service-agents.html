Monovus Launch for Customer Service Agents

Put your customer service agent in front of customers, with answers you can measure, escalation that works and a cost per conversation you can budget.

The problem

What usually stops this kind of agent going live.

A wrong answer is public

One bad answer reaches a customer, a screenshot travels, and the launch stops. Nobody can say how often it happens.

Escalation is an afterthought

The agent doesn't know when to hand off, or the hand-off loses the conversation, so the customer starts again with a person.

Cost grows with every conversation

Inference cost scales with volume, and long conversations and retries make the monthly bill hard to predict.

Approach

Assess, harden, launch.

Three phases, typically 3 to 4 weeks for one agent, at a fixed fee agreed in writing before the first day.

Week 1

Assess

  • Score the agent against the eight dimensions and this checklist
  • Build a golden set from 50 to 100 real conversations, including complaints and refund requests
  • Turn on tracing and measure today's cost per conversation
  • Agree the owner, the target and the hand-off rules

Weeks 2 to 3

Harden

  • Accuracy, policy and tone evaluations wired in as a release gate
  • Account actions behind verified identity, enforced outside the prompt
  • Escalation tested as its own scenario set, with the conversation passed on
  • Budgets, turn limits and model routing

Final week

Launch

  • Staged rollout, with a one-step switch back to people
  • Dashboards your support leads can read
  • Runbook, evidence pack and before-and-after scorecard
  • Handover to the team that will own the agent

The checklist

The eight dimensions, applied to customer service agents.

The checks we work through on every engagement of this kind. Use them on your own agent: each unticked line is a risk someone should own.

V · value

A clear owner and a measurable payoff

  • A named owner in support operations, accountable for the agent's results
  • One target, such as conversations resolved without a person, against today's baseline
  • A quality floor so the target can't be met with bad answers, such as repeat contacts within seven days

C1 · risk

Quality you can measure

  • A golden set from real transcripts: routine questions, complaints, refund requests, off-topic and abusive messages
  • Accuracy, policy and tone scored on every release; a lower score blocks it
  • The same tests in every language the agent answers in

C2 · risk

Every decision visible

  • Every conversation traced: articles retrieved, tools called, the answer given
  • Alarms on error rate and on a sudden rise in escalations
  • A way to find every conversation that quoted a policy you have since changed

C3 · cost

Predictable cost

  • Cost per conversation and per resolved conversation, reported weekly
  • Turn and token limits, so a looping conversation ends
  • Budgets with alarms at 50%, 80% and 100%, and simple turns on a smaller model

C4 · risk

The agent can only do what it should

  • Refunds, cancellations and account changes only after the customer is verified, enforced outside the prompt
  • Tools read-only unless an action is explicitly approved
  • No promises of compensation or exceptions your policy doesn't allow

C5 · risk

Access you can account for

  • Account lookups made as the verified customer, never with an admin key
  • Ticketing and CRM credentials in a secrets manager, rotated
  • Personal details masked in logs where the trace doesn't need them

C6 · risk

A fast way back

  • Prompts, knowledge sources and tool definitions under version control
  • One step routes every conversation to people
  • A rollback rehearsed before launch

C7 · risk

Ready for incidents and audits

  • A runbook for wrong answers at scale, an outage and a same-day policy change
  • Named owners for the agent, the knowledge and the escalation queue
  • An evidence pack: what the agent sees, what it can do and how it was tested

Questions

About customer service agents.

Do we need to replace our chat or contact-centre platform?

No. We work with the channels and ticketing system you already use. The agent reaches them through tools you approve, and nothing about how customers contact you has to change.

Can the agent issue refunds or change accounts?

Only after the customer is verified, and only the actions you approve. Those rules are enforced by a policy layer outside the prompt, so a clever message can't talk the agent past them.

How do you test tone and policy?

Every answer in the golden set is scored for accuracy, for following your policies and for tone, before each release. A release that scores worse than the last one doesn't ship.

What happens when the agent doesn't know?

It says so and hands the customer to a person with the conversation attached. Hand-off is tested as its own set of scenarios, not left to chance.

Can it answer in Chinese?

We work in English, Mandarin and Cantonese, so we can read your transcripts and build tests in each language the agent serves.

What will each conversation cost?

It depends on the model, the conversation length and the volume. We measure it in the first week and set budgets and limits before rollout, so the number is known before you scale.
