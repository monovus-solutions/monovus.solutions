Monovus Launch for Document Processing

Move documents from inbox to system of record with measured accuracy, a person in the loop where it matters and a full audit trail.

The problem

What usually stops this kind of agent going live.

Nobody knows the error rate

A demo on twenty clean PDFs says nothing about scans, handwriting and unusual layouts.

It writes to systems of record

A wrong amount posted to your finance system is expensive to find and to reverse.

Exceptions have no home

Documents the agent can't handle pile up with no queue, no owner and no deadline.

Approach

Assess, harden, launch.

Three phases, typically 3 to 4 weeks for one agent, at a fixed fee agreed in writing before the first day.

Week 1

Assess

  • Score the agent against the eight dimensions and this checklist
  • Build a test set of real documents across layouts, scan quality and languages
  • Measure field accuracy and cost per document today
  • Map every system the agent writes to, and agree approval thresholds

Weeks 2 to 3

Harden

  • Field-level accuracy tests as a release gate
  • Writes through narrow tools, with approval above agreed thresholds
  • A review queue for low-confidence documents, with an owner
  • Budgets, page limits, and batch or real-time processing chosen on cost

Final week

Launch

  • Run alongside the manual process, then switch over
  • A daily accuracy sample checked by a person
  • Runbook, evidence pack and before-and-after scorecard
  • Handover to the operations team

The checklist

The eight dimensions, applied to document processing.

The checks we work through on every engagement of this kind. Use them on your own agent: each unticked line is a risk someone should own.

V · value

A clear owner and a measurable payoff

  • An owner in the operations team that handles the documents today
  • One target, such as documents processed without a person, or cycle time, against a baseline
  • Cost per document compared with the manual process

C1 · risk

Quality you can measure

  • A test set of real documents across layouts, scan quality and languages
  • Accuracy measured per field, such as amount, date and supplier, not per document
  • The tests rerun before any model, prompt or schema change

C2 · risk

Every decision visible

  • Every document traced: what was extracted, with what confidence and where it was written
  • Alarms on review-queue depth and on processing failures
  • A daily sample checked by a person, with the results recorded

C3 · cost

Predictable cost

  • Cost per document and per page
  • Very large or unusual documents capped or routed to a person
  • Budgets with alarms, and batch processing where real time isn't needed

C4 · risk

The agent can only do what it should

  • Writes through narrow tools, such as 'create draft invoice', never 'post payment'
  • Low confidence, or amounts above a threshold, go to a person for approval
  • No deletes, and no changes to records the agent didn't create

C5 · risk

Access you can account for

  • A named identity for each system the agent writes to, with only the permissions it needs
  • System credentials in a secrets manager, rotated
  • Documents encrypted at rest and kept only as long as your retention policy allows

C6 · risk

A fast way back

  • Prompts, schemas and settings versioned
  • A batch can be reprocessed after a fix
  • Drafts can be withdrawn before anything is posted

C7 · risk

Ready for incidents and audits

  • A runbook for a bad batch, and for a supplier changing its document format
  • Named owners for the review queue and for each target system
  • An evidence pack for finance and audit: accuracy, thresholds and a record of every write

Questions

About document processing.

Which documents can it handle?

Invoices, claims, forms, contracts and correspondence, as PDFs, scans or photos. We test on your own documents in the first week, including the worst scans, before saying anything about accuracy.

How accurate will it be?

We don't quote a number before measuring. The first week gives field-level accuracy on your documents, and the thresholds for human review are set from that measurement.

Does a person still check documents?

Where it matters. Low-confidence fields and amounts above your thresholds go to a review queue, and the rest is sampled daily.

Can it post directly to our finance system?

It can create drafts or records through narrow tools you approve. Posting and payment stay with a person unless you decide otherwise, in writing.

What does each document cost?

It depends on pages, layout and model. We measure it in the first week and compare it with your manual cost before you scale.

Where are the documents kept?

In your AWS account, encrypted, for as long as your retention policy says. We don't copy them into systems of ours.
