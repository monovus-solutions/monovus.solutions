About

One person. One problem.

Monovus is a solo operation based in Toronto.

I run 15+ AI agents in production. They handle my inbox, write my status updates, monitor my infrastructure, file my expenses, and prep my meetings. It took me months to build this stack — I have a background in DevOps and infrastructure engineering.

Most people don't have that background. They hear "AI can automate your work" and then hit a wall of Docker, SSH keys, cron jobs, and YAML files. The gap between "AI can do this" and "AI is doing this for me" is an infrastructure gap, not an intelligence gap.

The thesis

AI assistants need ops, not just prompts. The hard part isn't telling the AI what to do — it's keeping it running reliably, monitoring what it does, and knowing when to intervene. That's ops work. Most people shouldn't have to learn ops to benefit from AI.

What Monovus does

We install and operate AI assistants for non-technical operators. You tell us what's eating your time. We set up the agents, the automations, and the monitoring. You get daily check-ins — what it did, what it's about to do, what needs your approval.

One afternoon to install. One glance a day to operate.
