I'm Alex Shaw. I've spent more than fifteen years in AWS presales and solution architecture, helping companies get from a working idea to a system they trust. Monovus is my own practice, and it does one thing: it gets AI agents on AWS safely into production.

Why so narrow

Building an agent is now the easy part, and plenty of firms will do it. Getting one into production, and keeping it there, is where pilots stall and where large firms see the work as too small. A fixed two-week sprint on one agent is a size one experienced person can deliver well, and a size you can judge me on.

Monovus is new. I have no client stories yet and I won't invent any. You'll see the scorecard on your own agent before you pay for anything.
