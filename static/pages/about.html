Company

An AWS-focused consultancy for one problem: getting AI agents into production.

Monovus is based in Toronto and works with companies across North America. We take AI agents that work in a demo and make them something the business can measure, afford and defend.

Why Monovus exists

One problem, done properly.

Building an agent is now the easy part, and many firms will do it. Getting one into production, and keeping it there, is where pilots stall, and it is work that often falls between the team that built the agent and the teams that run production systems.

We do that one thing, on AWS only, with a published method and fixed prices. A fixed engagement on one agent is a size we can deliver well, and a size you can judge us on.

Monovus is a new company. Instead of logos, we offer a published framework, fixed prices and a free assessment you can judge us by before you spend anything.

What we believe

Five principles.

  • Measure before you trustAn agent is ready when the numbers say so, not when the demo goes well.
  • Controls belong outside the promptA prompt is a request. Rules that matter are enforced by policy and permissions.
  • Your account, your codeEverything runs in your AWS account and lands in your repositories.
  • Fixed scope beats open-ended hoursA written scope and a fixed fee keep both sides honest.
  • Say no when the answer is noIf production is the wrong goal, we say so before you spend anything.

Plain terms

What we won't do.

  • We won't sell you a platform. AgentCore already has the parts; we configure them and prove they work on your agent.
  • We won't start work without a written scope and a fixed fee, and we won't bill by the hour.
  • We won't be your 24/7 pager. Your team owns on-call, and leaves with the alarms, runbook and rollback to do it.
  • We won't build anything only we can maintain. If your team can't run it, it isn't finished.
  • We won't call an agent ready when it isn't. If it shouldn't go to production yet, the scorecard says so in writing.
  • We won't turn a five-minute fix into a project, and we won't mark up your AWS bill.
