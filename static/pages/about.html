About

A senior-led boutique, by design.

Monovus is an AWS-focused AI agent consultancy based in Toronto. The person who scopes your work is the person who delivers it, so nothing is handed to a junior team after the sale.

Why narrow

One problem, done properly.

Building an agent is now the easy part, and many firms will do it. Getting one into production, and keeping it there, is where pilots stall and where large firms often see the work as too small.

We do that one thing. A fixed engagement on one agent is a size a senior practitioner can deliver well, and a size you can judge us on.

Monovus is a new practice. Instead of logos, we offer a free assessment you can judge us by, and a fixed fee quoted before any work starts.

Plain terms

What we won't do.

  • We won't sell you a platform. AgentCore already has the parts; we configure them and prove they work on your agent.
  • We won't hand your work to a junior team after the sale. The person who scopes it delivers it.
  • We won't be your 24/7 pager. Your team owns on-call, and leaves with the alarms, runbook and rollback to do it.
  • We won't build anything only we can maintain. If your team can't run it, it isn't finished.
  • We won't call an agent ready when it isn't. If it shouldn't go to production yet, the scorecard says so in writing.
  • We won't turn a five-minute fix into a project, and we won't mark up your AWS bill.
