Install in an afternoon
How it works

Three steps. One afternoon.

You don't need to learn infrastructure. You just need to decide what you want automated.

01

Book a 30-minute call

Tell us what's eating your time. Inbox? Reporting? Research? We'll scope exactly what your AI assistant should handle.

No prep needed. Just bring your frustrations.

02

We install your AI stack

We set up the agents, the automations, and the monitoring — on your infrastructure or ours. You watch, ask questions, learn the controls.

Typical install: 2–4 hours. You're running by sundown.

03

Daily check-ins, not babysitting

Your AI sends you a morning brief: what it did overnight, what it's about to do, what needs your approval. You stay in control without doing the work.

One glance. One approval. Back to your day.

A typical first week

Day 130-min scoping call. We agree on what to automate first.
Day 1 (PM)Install session. Your AI stack is live by evening.
Day 2First morning brief arrives. You see what it did overnight.
Day 3–5We tune the rules together. Less noise, more signal.
Day 7Check-in call. You're running independently.
