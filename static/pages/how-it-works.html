The Pilot-to-Production Sprint

Week by week.

Typically 2 to 4 weeks on one agent that already works in a demo. A smaller agent compresses weeks 2 and 3. The scope and fee are fixed in writing before day one.

Week 1 · Assess

Baseline and value

  • Access through an IAM role you control
  • Tools, data and permissions mapped
  • Tracing switched on
  • Owner and target agreed

Result: A scorecard of the eight dimensions as they stand.

Week 2 · Harden

Quality and visibility

  • A golden set of at least 25 real cases
  • Evaluations wired into releases
  • Dashboards and an error alarm

Result: A release that scores worse than the last one can't ship.

Week 3 · Harden

Cost, policy and identity

  • Cost per task measured
  • Budget alarms at 50%, 80% and 100%
  • Policy on every tool call
  • Agent identity and secrets in a vault

Result: The agent can only do what it should, at a cost you know.

Week 4 · Launch

The way back, and handover

  • A rollback rehearsed
  • Runbook and evidence pack
  • Before-and-after scorecard
  • Ranked backlog of what is left

Result: Your team runs the agent without us.

What we need from you

Four things, agreed up front.

  • One named ownerAccountable for the agent's results on your side.
  • About two hours a week of an engineerSomeone who knows the agent and its systems.
  • Real examplesConversations or tasks to build the golden set from.
  • One AWS accountReached through a role you create and can revoke.
