Questions

What counts as an "agent"?

One automation workflow. The inbox triager is one agent. The expense filer is another. Each does one job well.

Can I switch from Solo to Managed?

Yes. We migrate your self-hosted setup to our infrastructure in a single session. No data loss, no downtime.

What infrastructure do you use?

Linux VMs with Docker, Prometheus monitoring, Tailscale networking, and encrypted backups. Standard, auditable, no vendor lock-in.

Do I need to provide API keys?

Yes — you bring your own AI provider keys. We never store or access them directly. They stay on your infrastructure.

What if I need more than 5 agents?

Email us. We'll scope a custom plan based on your workload.
