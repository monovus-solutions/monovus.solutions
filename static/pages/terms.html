The backup application

Monovus operates a private application, registered with Google as NAS Offsite Backup, which copies encrypted backup archives from a machine you control into a Google Drive account you control.

It is a tool, not a managed service

The application runs on your hardware, authenticates as you, and writes to your storage. Monovus does not monitor your backups, does not receive alerts when they fail, and has no server that holds your data. If you want someone watching it, that is a separate paid engagement.

What stays your responsibility

  • Verifying restores. A backup that has never been restored is a hypothesis. Test yours.
  • Keeping more than one copy. One offsite copy is not a backup strategy. Keep at least one other.
  • Your encryption key. Archives are encrypted before upload and the key never leaves your machine. Lose the key and the backup is permanently unreadable — by you, by us, and by Google. We cannot recover it.
  • Your Google account. Its storage limits, quotas, billing, and terms are between you and Google.
