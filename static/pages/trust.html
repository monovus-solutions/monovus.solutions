Trust & security

How we work with your data, your accounts and your agents.

These are working practices, written down so you can hold us to them. They apply to every engagement, and the written scope can add to them.

Access

Your account, your access.

  • We work inside your AWS accountThrough an IAM role you create, scoped to the engagement, which you can revoke at any time.
  • Least privilege, per engagementWe ask only for the access the written scope needs, and say why each permission is needed.
  • MFA on every account we useMulti-factor authentication on every account we use to reach your systems, and on every account of ours that holds your information.
  • No copies of your dataYour data stays in your account. We don't copy it into systems of ours.
  • An NDA before discoveryOn request, we sign your NDA, or offer ours, before you share any detail of the agent.
  • Access removed at the endWhen an engagement ends, we ask you to delete the role, and confirm in writing that we hold nothing of yours.

Ownership

You own everything we produce.

  • Code, prompts, test sets and documentsEverything we write for you is yours, with nothing to license from us.
  • Delivered as codeInfrastructure, policies, evaluation suites and dashboards land in your repositories, reviewed with your team.
  • No proprietary runtimeThe agent runs on AWS services in your account, so it keeps running if we stop working together.

How we build

Published standards, applied the same way.

We align our work with the AWS Well-Architected Framework and its Generative AI Lens, AWS's published guidance for generative AI workloads.

Every agent is measured against the eight dimensions of the Monovus Production Readiness Framework, before and after, and every change we make is versioned and reviewed.

Each engagement ends with an evidence pack: what the agent can reach, what it can do, how it was tested and who owns it. It is written for your security reviewers, customers and auditors.

Responsible AI

People stay in charge.

  • Human approval for high-impact actionsRefunds, payments, account changes and anything hard to undo need a person's approval, unless you decide otherwise in writing.
  • No fully autonomous high-impact actionsWe don't build agents that take consequential actions with no one able to approve or stop them.
  • Honest by designBy default, agents tell people they are talking to an AI, say when they don't know, and offer a way to reach a person.
  • Tested before release, watched afterEvaluations gate every release, and traces show what the agent did and why.
  • A straight answerIf an agent shouldn't go to production yet, the scorecard says so, in writing.

The free assessment
