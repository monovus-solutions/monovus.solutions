When teams call

The pilot works.
Something else doesn't.

The best fit is a company of roughly 50 to 1,000 people, or a software vendor, with an agent on AWS that works in a demo and isn't trusted in production. Usually one of these happened first.

The bill surprised someone

Inference cost grew faster than usage and nobody can say which turns are expensive. Controls 2 and 3 answer that in the first week.

It said something it shouldn't

A wrong or off-policy answer reached a customer or a colleague. Tracing finds why; evals stop the same class of mistake shipping again.

A security review is coming

Security, a customer or an auditor wants to know what the agent can touch and how you would know if it went wrong. Controls 4 to 7 are that evidence.

Leadership is asking about AI risk

Someone wants a straight answer on whether the pilot can go live. The before-and-after scorecard is that answer, in writing.

When I'm the wrong call

  • You don't have an agent yet and want a strategy for what to do with AI.
  • You need a multi-agent platform built from scratch.
  • Your procurement requires the vendor to hold SOC 2.
  • Your agent runs somewhere other than AWS.

If that's you, tell me anyway. I'll point you to someone better suited if I can.

In your language

I work in English, Mandarin and Cantonese. If your agent answers customers in Chinese, I can read its transcripts and build its golden set without a translator.
