By function

Five kinds of agent we take to production.

Customer service and support

Answers customers, resolves tickets, hands off to people.

What usually stops it: A wrong answer in front of a customer, or a bill that grew with every conversation.

What production needs most: Quality you can measure; Every decision visible; Predictable cost.

Internal knowledge and employee help

Answers staff questions from policies, wikis and documents.

What usually stops it: Answers nobody can trace to a source, and access to documents some staff shouldn't see.

What production needs most: Quality you can measure; The agent can only do what it should; Access you can account for.

Document processing and back office

Reads, checks and routes invoices, claims, forms and contracts.

What usually stops it: No measured error rate, and write access to systems of record.

What production needs most: Quality you can measure; The agent can only do what it should; A fast way back.

Sales and marketing

Researches accounts, drafts outreach, qualifies leads.

What usually stops it: Unclear payback, and an agent that can send things on the company's behalf.

What production needs most: A clear owner and a measurable payoff; Predictable cost; The agent can only do what it should.

AI features inside your product

An agent your own customers use, inside your software.

What usually stops it: Customer security reviews, per-tenant cost, and releases that change behaviour.

What production needs most: Quality you can measure; Predictable cost; Ready for incidents and audits.

When teams call

Usually, one of these happened first.

  • The bill surprised someoneInference cost grew faster than usage, and nobody can say which turns are expensive.
  • It said something it shouldn'tA wrong or off-policy answer reached a customer or a colleague.
  • A security review is comingSecurity, a customer or an auditor wants to know what the agent can touch.
  • Leadership is asking about AI riskSomeone needs a straight answer on whether the pilot can go live.
  • The launch date is slippingThe demo has been ready for months, and production keeps moving.
